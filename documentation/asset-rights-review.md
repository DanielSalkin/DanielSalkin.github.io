Deere's published policy requires prior written approval for logo use, subject to uses authorized by applicable law. No permission has been supplied; the logo and recoloring were removed. This is not a determination that every factual portfolio use would be unlawful.

This is not an unrestricted artwork or recoloring license. No permission or case-specific legal clearance was established; the logo and recoloring were removed.
- Illini Electric Motorsports: https://legacy.illinielectricmotorsports.com/ . Public availability of the team logo does not establish an open license. No permission to republish/recolor was verified; the standalone logo was removed.
- MorTorq / FRC 1515: https://team1515.com/ and https://www.thebluealliance.com/team/1515/2025 . Availability of the team avatar on TBA does not establish permission to republish/recolor the artwork. The standalone avatar was removed.

Names remain in ordinary typography to factually describe Daniel's experience, with official-site links. This avoids standalone decorative logo reproduction and recoloring. It is not a blanket legal clearance. To restore logos, obtain rights covering portfolio display and any intended color modifications, or get case-specific legal advice supporting that use. No organizations have been contacted.
